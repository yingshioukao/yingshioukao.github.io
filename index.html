About

About Me

I am an Adjunct Assistant Professor in the Department of Political Science at Soochow University, Taiwan. My research examines how democratic institutions, political actors, and civil society shape accountability and political representation.

I use quantitative, qualitative, and experimental methods, with a particular focus on Japan and Taiwan. I hold a Ph.D. in Political Science from Soochow University and a Ph.D. in Chinese Studies from Aichi University, Japan. Previously, I held postdoctoral positions at Soochow University and Academia Sinica.

Beyond academia, I have conducted policy research for the Westminster Foundation for Democracy and contributed to the independent review of Taiwan's Open Parliament Action Plan.
