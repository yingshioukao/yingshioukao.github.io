Democratic Accountability & Comparative Politics

I study how democratic institutions, political actors, and civil society shape accountability and political representation, with a particular focus on Japan and Taiwan.

My research spans parliamentary institutions, political behavior, and civil society, combining comparative perspectives with empirical research.
