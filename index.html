About

About Me

I am an Adjunct Assistant Professor in the Department of Political Science at Soochow University, Taiwan. My research focuses on democratic accountability, particularly legislative institutions, political representation, and civil society.

My work examines how political institutions, elected representatives, and citizens shape democratic governance. I employ quantitative, qualitative, and experimental approaches, with a regional focus on Japan and Taiwan.

I hold a Ph.D. in Political Science from Soochow University and a Ph.D. in Chinese Studies from Aichi University, Japan. Previously, I held postdoctoral research positions at Soochow University and Academia Sinica.

Beyond academia, I have conducted policy research with the Westminster Foundation for Democracy and contributed to the independent review of Taiwan's Open Parliament Action Plan.
